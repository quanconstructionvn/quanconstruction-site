Land and House Registration Fee 2026: 0.5% Rate and Exemptions

Quan Construction · 21/10/2026

When registering land use rights or house ownership (sale, gift, inheritance, registration of a newly built house, etc.), the registrant must pay a registration fee (lệ phí trước bạ), unless exempt. Current rules are in Decree 10/2022/ND-CP and its amending decrees, consolidated in Consolidated Document 33/VBHN-BTC.

1. Rate

Under Article 8: the registration fee rate for houses and land is 0.5%.

2. Value used to calculate the fee

  • Land: the land price in the Land Price Table issued by the provincial or city People's Committee, at the time of declaration (Article 7).
  • Houses: the price issued by the provincial or city People's Committee under construction law, at the time of declaration.
  • Some special cases (auction purchases, apartments, state-owned houses, etc.) have their own calculation under the same Article 7.

Reference formula: Fee = Area × Unit price per the provincial People's Committee price table × 0.5%. Example: for a 100 m² plot with a price table rate of VND 5,000,000/m², the land fee = 100 × 5,000,000 × 0.5% = VND 2,500,000 (not including the house, if any).

3. Common exemptions

Under Article 10, the fee is exempt for houses and land received by inheritance or as a gift between: husband and wife; biological parents and biological children; adoptive parents and adopted children; parents-in-law and daughter-in-law; parents-in-law and son-in-law; paternal or maternal grandparents and grandchildren; and siblings. There are also other cases, such as division of a household's common property and the merged property of spouses.

4. Note on new documents

Decree 202/2026/ND-CP, which amends Decree 10/2022/ND-CP, takes effect from 1 March 2027. For transactions from that date, check the new rules again.

This article is for reference only. The exact amount is notified by the tax authority upon declaration; land and house price tables differ by province.
