Designing Your Own House in Vietnam 2026: When to Hire a Designer

Quan Construction · 28/10/2026

Many families want to draw the house themselves, or ask an acquaintance to draw it, to save money. The law allows this, but only for small houses. From 1 July 2026, the rules are in Article 10 of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP on construction management of private houses. This article summarizes what homeowners need to know, with links to the original text.

1. Which houses may the owner design themselves?

Under Point a, Clause 2, Article 10, the house owner may organize the design themselves when the house meets all 4 criteria at the same time:

  • Fewer than 3 floors;
  • No basement;
  • Total construction floor area under 250 m²;
  • Height under 12 m.

Exceeding even one criterion (for example, a 2-floor house with a total floor area of 260 m², or one with a basement) takes the house out of the self-design case.

2. When must a design firm be hired?

  • Houses of fewer than 7 floors or with 1 basement (other than the small houses in section 1): the design must be done by an organization or individual with the relevant expertise and experience as required by law (Point b, Clause 2, Article 10).
  • Houses of 7 floors or more, or with 2 or more basements: in addition to hiring an organization or individual with expertise and experience, the design documents must comply with technical standards and regulations on fire prevention and fighting (Point c, Clause 2, Article 10).

In practice, most 3-5 floor townhouses in urban areas fall into the group that must hire a design firm.

3. What must self-designed houses still ensure?

Being allowed to design it yourself does not mean drawing freely. Under Clauses 1 and 6, Article 10, the design and construction of a private house must comply with technical standards and regulations; ensure safety for people, property, neighboring works and the surrounding environment; and comply with fire prevention and fighting and environmental protection laws.

If the house must obtain a building permit, under Clause 3, Article 60 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP, the drawing set submitted with the application consists of:

  1. A site plan of the building on the plot with a location diagram of the building;
  2. Floor plans, elevations and main sections;
  3. A foundation plan and foundation section with a diagram of external water supply, drainage and electricity connections;
  4. A written undertaking to ensure the safety of adjoining buildings.

For the full application, filing venue and time limits, see Procedure for a private house building permit 2026.

4. Hiring a designer: note on architectural practice certificates

Under Article 19 of the Law on Architecture, architectural design of works is a type of architectural service. Under Article 21 of the Law on Architecture, the individual who leads the architectural design, the individual responsible for architectural expertise in an architectural practice organization, and architects practicing as individuals must hold an architectural practice certificate; a person without a certificate may only participate within an architectural practice organization or cooperate with a practicing architect.

When signing a design contract, the homeowner should ask the firm to identify the lead designer and their practice certificate. Note: the 2019 Law on Architecture is currently partly no longer in force, so amending documents should also be checked when applying it.

5. What is the penalty for letting an unqualified person design?

Under Point c, Clause 2, Article 22 of Decree 339/2026/ND-CP (effective from 26 August 2026), an investor who lets an organization or individual without expertise and experience carry out design, construction or construction supervision of a private house as required is fined VND 20,000,000 to 40,000,000. Under Clause 4, Article 5, this is the level for organizations; a homeowner who is an individual is fined half, i.e. VND 10,000,000 to 20,000,000.

In addition, if the building is under construction, the homeowner is ordered to arrange an organization or individual with expertise and experience to carry out the design, construction and supervision (a remedial measure under the same Article 22).

6. After design: signage and construction supervision

  • The homeowner must install a project sign at the site entrance, clearly stating the homeowner's name, address and contact phone number and the other required content (Point a, Clause 3, Article 10). Failing to install it, or installing it with missing content, may be fined under Point b, Clause 1, Article 22 of Decree 339/2026/ND-CP (VND 10-20 million at organization level; individuals half).
  • The homeowner organizes construction and construction supervision and is responsible for safety toward neighboring works; for houses of 7 floors or more or with 2 or more basements, construction and supervision are mandatorily under Decree 207/2026/ND-CP (Points b and c, Clause 3, Article 10).
  • If the function is converted (for example, to a house combined with business) together with repair or renovation, the design must conform to the standards for the new function (Clause 5, Article 10).
This article is only a summary for reference and does not replace the legal documents. For a house close to the thresholds of 3 floors, 250 m² or 12 m, have a qualified design firm check in advance to avoid being required to redo the application or being penalized.
