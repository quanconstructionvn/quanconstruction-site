Conditions to Start Building a House in Vietnam 2026

Quan Construction · 27/10/2026

Choosing an auspicious groundbreaking date is not enough: under the law, a house may start construction only when it meets the conditions of Article 48 of the Construction Law 2025 (effective from 1 July 2026). The good news for homeowners is that private houses of households and individuals are subject to much simpler conditions than other works.

1. A private house needs only 2 conditions

For ordinary works, Clause 1, Article 48 requires 5 conditions: a construction site; a building permit; approved construction drawing design; a signed contract with a contractor; and a notice of commencement already sent.

For a private house of a household or individual, Clause 3, Article 48 requires only:

  1. A building permit as provided in Article 43 of the Construction Law 2025 (unless exempt from a permit);
  2. Construction on lawful residential land under land law.

2. The building permit condition

Most houses in wards and planned residential areas must have a permit before starting construction. A private house of fewer than 7 floors with a total floor area under 500 m², located outside areas covered by planning and architectural management regulations, is exempt from a permit under Clause 2, Article 43.

See also: Which private houses are exempt from a building permit? and Procedure for a private house building permit 2026.

3. The lawful residential land condition

The house must be on residential land. If the land is agricultural land, garden land, etc., the land use purpose must first be converted. When applying for a permit, the lawful land documents are listed in Article 55 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP, the most common being:

  • A land use right certificate (sổ đỏ, sổ hồng) issued in any period;
  • Documents qualifying for a certificate under Article 137 of the Land Law 2024;
  • A land allocation decision or decision permitting a change of land use purpose (for cases from 1 July 2004 that do not yet have a certificate).

4. Must a private house send a notice of commencement?

No. Point đ, Clause 1, Article 48 states that a written or online notice of commencement to the local construction authority is required except for private houses of households and individuals. Article 12 of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP likewise requires a commencement notice (in the form in Appendix V) only for works under Clause 1, Article 48, which does not include private houses.

5. What the homeowner must do when construction begins

Under Article 10 of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP:

  • Install a project sign at the site entrance, clearly stating the homeowner's name, address and phone number; the name and scale of the building; the start and completion dates; and a perspective drawing (under Clause 1, Article 13).
  • Design by the right person: the homeowner may design the house personally only if it has fewer than 3 floors, no basement, a total floor area under 250 m² and a height under 12 m, all at once; otherwise it must be done by an organization or individual with the relevant expertise and experience.
  • Bear responsibility for safety: the homeowner organizes construction and construction supervision and is responsible for safety of the building, neighboring buildings and the environment. Houses of 7 floors or more, or with 2 or more basements, must organize construction and supervision under Decree 207/2026/ND-CP.

6. What is the penalty for starting before the conditions are met?

From 26 August 2026, penalties are imposed under Decree 339/2026/ND-CP. The fines below are the levels applicable to organizations; individuals are fined half (Clause 4, Article 5).

  • Starting construction without a permit where one is required: Clause 3, Article 21 refers to sanctions under Clause 7, Article 25 - a fine of VND 60,000,000 - 80,000,000 for a private house (VND 80,000,000 - 100,000,000 if within a relic or heritage protection zone).
  • Failing to display the building permit at the site: a fine of VND 5,000,000 - 10,000,000 for a private house (Clause 2, Article 25).
  • Failing to install a project sign, or a sign with missing content: a fine of VND 10,000,000 - 20,000,000 and an order to install it (Point b, Clause 1, Article 22).
  • Allowing a person without the required expertise and experience to design, build or supervise a private house: a fine of VND 20,000,000 - 40,000,000 (Point c, Clause 2, Article 22).

Quick checklist before groundbreaking day:

  1. The land is already residential land and has lawful documents;
  2. The building permit has been obtained, or exemption has been firmly established;
  3. The drawings are prepared by the right person (self-design only if all 4 criteria above are met);
  4. The project sign and a copy of the permit are ready to display at the site;
  5. There is a plan to ensure safety for adjoining houses.
This article is only a summary for reference and does not replace the legal documents. Specific fines are decided by the competent person case by case; if you are unsure whether your house needs a permit, ask the commune or ward People's Committee where you are building.
