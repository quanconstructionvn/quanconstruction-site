What we do

Business Areas

Residential and industrial construction, consulting and design, estimating; supply and installation of aluminium and glass.

Core business

Building from foundation to finish

From homes to factories, every project is closely controlled for quality, schedule and cost.

Construction

Houses, factories and other civil and industrial buildings.

  • Structural and finishing works
  • Construction to approved drawings
  • Schedule and quality commitments in the contract

Consulting, design & permits

Solution consulting, design and building-permit procedures.

  • Site and condition survey
  • Design in line with current regulations
  • Building-permit dossiers and procedures

Estimating & final settlement

Quantity take-off from drawings, cost estimates, tender dossiers and project settlement.

  • Quantity take-off (BOQ)
  • Unit-rate analysis and variation control

Finishing, repair & renovation

Interior and exterior finishing, and renovation of homes, offices and shops.

  • Quick survey and quotation
  • Warranty after handover
