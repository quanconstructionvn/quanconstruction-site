This page gathers 104 forms taken from the appendices of decrees and circulars currently in force; the forms themselves are in Vietnamese, as issued under Vietnamese law. Each form comes in 3 versions, PDF, Word and Excel, to print or to complete on a computer. The content of each form is reproduced verbatim from the legal document, with nothing added or removed; the form number, appendix and source document are stated on each form.

For some documents that the law does not provide a form template for but only prescribes mandatory content (for example, the record of acceptance of work items), the form is presented in line with, and consists only of, that mandatory content, with a note at the top of the form: “The law does not issue a template - presented according to the mandatory content in …”.
