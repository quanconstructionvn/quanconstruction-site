Advance and payment dossiers for public investment capital: Decree 254/2025

Applies to tasks and projects using public investment capital - prepared by the investor and sent to the payment authority (State Treasury).

Decree 254/2025/ND-CP (signed on 26 September 2025, effective from the date of signing) governs the management and payment of public investment capital and replaces Decree 99/2021/ND-CP (Article 56). Final settlement of completed-project capital is now carried out under Decree 193/2026/ND-CP (see QS - payment and final settlement); the advance payment and payment rules below continue to apply under Decree 254/2025.

These are dossiers that the investor sends to the payment authority (the State Treasury - Article 4). The dossier a contractor sends to the investor to be paid under a contract is governed by Article 29 of Decree 210/2026/ND-CP.

1. Legal dossier (sent the first time, or when adjusted)

No.DocumentNotes
1Annual public investment plan assigned by the competent authority
2Decision/document assigning the task; decision approving the investment project and any adjustment decision (if any)
3Document permitting self-performance; document assigning work or internal contractingWhere work is self-performed or contracted internally
4Contract information summary sheet - Form No. 02.a/TTPrepared by the investor on the basis of a contract that has taken effect; the investor is responsible for its content
5Cost estimate information summary sheet - Form No. 02.b/TT; annual revenue and expenditure estimate of the investor or PMU (if any)Work not performed under a contract
6Compensation, support and resettlement information summary sheet - Form No. 02.c/TTWhere there is compensation or site clearance
7Decision establishing the unit; decision granting financial autonomyPMU, organization carrying out compensation and site clearance

Basis: Clause 1, Article 8. A legal dossier already sent before the decree took effect does not need to be resubmitted; it is supplemented only when there are changes (Clause 1, Article 54).

2. Advance payment dossier (sent with each advance request)

  1. Request for payment of capital - Form No. 04.a/TT.
  2. Withdrawal request - Form No. 05.a/TT.
  3. Contract advance payment guarantee (original or certified copy) where a guarantee is required.

Basis: Clause 2, Article 8. Principles for advance payment (Article 9):

  • An advance may be made only after the contract has taken effect and the investor has received the advance payment guarantee (if required); a construction contract involving site clearance must have a site clearance plan or a site handover record.
  • An advance payment guarantee is mandatory where the contract advance exceeds VND 1 billion; the guarantee value decreases as the advance is recovered and remains valid until fully recovered. No guarantee is required for: advances of VND 1 billion or less (by agreement), self-performance, hiring project management consultants, work not under a contract, compensation and site clearance (except for building works), and project management costs.
  • The contract advance rate for construction contracts complies with construction law (see Article 12 of Decree 210/2026/ND-CP) and must not exceed the amount in the contract/estimate information summary sheet or the annual capital plan.
  • Recovery of the advance is made through successive payments and must be completed in full when disbursement reaches 80% of the contract or estimate value; advances for compensation, support and resettlement must be processed for payment and recovery no later than 30 days from the date of the advance.
  • Overdue advances: if an advance has not been recovered 3 months after the time by which it had to be fully recovered under the contract (or if use for a wrong purpose is detected), the investor, together with the payment authority, leads recovery and may request the credit institution to perform its guarantee.

3. Dossier for payment of completed work volumes

CaseDossier sent to the payment authority
Work performed under a contractForm No. 04.a/TT request for payment of capital; Form No. 05.a/TT withdrawal request; Form No. 04.b/TT request for recovery of advanced capital (if recovering); Form No. 03.a/TT sheet determining the value of completed work volumes
Compensation, support and resettlement04.a/TT; 05.a/TT; 04.b/TT (if recovering); Form No. 03.b/TT statement of the value of compensation, support and resettlement work
Other work not under a contract (including project management costs when managing 1 project)04.a/TT; 05.a/TT; 04.b/TT (if recovering); 03.a/TT
Project with approved final settlement but not yet fully paid04.a/TT; decision approving final settlement; 05.a/TT
Insurance contracts, appraisal fees, audit and final settlement approval costs04.a/TT; 05.a/TT
Project management costs when managing several projects04.a/TT; 05.a/TT; 04.b/TT (if recovering); decision approving final settlement or Form No. 09/QLDA project management cost allocation sheet + 03.a/TT

Basis: Clauses 3 and 6, Article 8. Payment under a contract must conform to the contract type, contract price and contract conditions; amounts retained for final settlement purposes (if agreed in the contract) are transferred to the investor's deposit account at the Treasury (Article 10). Disbursed capital must not exceed the total investment, the annual capital plan, or the contract/estimate value in the information summary sheets (Article 6).

4. Deadlines

  • The Treasury makes payment within 2 working days of receiving a complete dossier; advances within 1 working day (Clause 4, Article 12).
  • For a deficient or incorrect dossier, the payment authority rejects it and notifies the reason in writing once, within 2 working days at the latest (Clause 9, Article 8).
  • Annual plan capital may be advanced until the end of 31 December of the plan year (compensation and site clearance until 31 January of the following year); payment for accepted completed work volumes is made until 31 December, with the payment deadline running to the end of 31 January of the following year (Clauses 1 and 2, Article 12).

5. Periodic advance reports

Before the 5th of the first month of the following quarter, the investor prepares a report on advance payments and recovery of advances for each task and project (Form No. 08.a/SDTƯ), stating clearly the balance of overdue advances, and sends it to the payment authority, the superior agency and the financial agency (Point b, Clause 6, Article 9).

6. Forms

The forms system is in the Appendix to Decree 254/2025/ND-CP (Form 01.a/PB to 10/CT - list in the Appendix section). Our library currently has only the list and not the content of each form; the forms will be added to the Forms page (in PDF, Word and Excel) once the full text of the appendix has been loaded from the National Legal Portal.

This summary is for reference and applies only to tasks and projects using public investment capital. Decree 254/2025/ND-CP is partially no longer in force (final settlement of completed-project capital has been replaced by Decree 193/2026/ND-CP). Overseas public investment projects, State secret projects, emergency projects and National Target Programs have separate provisions in Articles 8 and 14-24. Also consult the guidance of the State Treasury office where you transact.
