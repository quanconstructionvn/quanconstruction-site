Warranty & After-sales

Item-by-item warranty and continued support after handover.

Warranty

Handover is not the end

Every Quan Construction project is warranted item by item for a clearly stated period, counted from the handover date. We stay with you so that your home remains as good as the first day.

10 yearsLoad-bearing structure: foundations, columns, load-bearing walls, floors, roof structure
5 yearsWaterproofing: roof structure, roof, exterior walls and exterior doors
2 yearsExterior and interior finishes and building systems
After-sales

Staying with you after handover

One point of contact

All feedback about your project is received at a single contact point and tracked until resolved.

Inspection & confirmation

Our technical staff inspect on site, confirm the cause and determine whether the warranty applies.

On-site remedy

Items within the warranty scope are rectified promptly and to the correct technical standard.
