Quick calculators: advance, security, warranty, contract penalties

Enter the figures and see results instantly – with legal minimums, caps and warnings when limits are exceeded.

The tools below calculate using the statutory formulas and rates currently in force. Many rates are mandatory only for public investment and PPP projects; for projects with other funding they are agreed by the parties in the contract. All figures are processed only on your device and are not sent anywhere.

1. Contract advance payment

Public investment and PPP projects: minimum advance payment under Clause 3, Article 12 of Decree 210/2026/ND-CP. Projects with other funding: as agreed by the parties.

2. Contract performance security

Public investment and PPP projects: 2% to 10% of the contract price (Point dd, Clause 2, Article 13 of Decree 210/2026/ND-CP).

3. Works warranty amount

Works funded by public investment capital or the State budget: at least 3% (special grade, grade I) or 5% (other grades) of the contract value; minimum warranty period of 24 or 12 months (Clauses 5 and 7, Article 32 of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP).

4. Contract penalties

Public investment and PPP projects: no more than 12% of the value of the breached part of the contract (Clause 3, Article 86 of Construction Law 2025). Commercial contracts under the Commercial Law: no more than 8% (Article 301). In addition to the penalty, compensation for damages may also be payable.

5. Late payment interest

Interest = overdue amount × annual interest rate × days overdue ÷ 365. The agreed interest rate may not exceed 20%/year; where interest is agreed but the rate is unclear and a dispute arises, the rate is 50% of the cap (Articles 357 and 468 of the Civil Code). The contract may provide a different calculation method.

6. Loan instalments (equal monthly principal + interest)

Annuity formula: A = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), r = annual interest rate ÷ 12. For estimation only; banks may calculate on a declining balance or with floating interest.

7. Personal income tax and registration fee on real estate transactions

Seller's personal income tax (PIT) = transfer price × 2% (Article 14 of PIT Law 109/2025/QH15). Registration fee (lệ phí trước bạ) on houses and land = fee calculation price × 0.5% (Clause 1, Article 8 of Decree 10/2022/ND-CP, consolidated text 33/VBHN-BTC). The calculation price may be the provincial land price table or registration fee price table if the contract price is lower; exemptions are not covered here.

Legal basis of the tools

  • Advance payment: Article 12 of Decree 210/2026/ND-CP (minimum rates at Points h, i, k, Clause 3; advance payment guarantee at Point c).
  • Contract performance security: Article 83 of Construction Law 2025; Point dd, Clause 2, Article 13 of Decree 210/2026/ND-CP.
  • Warranty: Clauses 5 and 7, Article 32 of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP.
  • Contract penalties: Clause 3, Article 86 of Construction Law 2025; Article 301 of Commercial Law 2005.
  • Late payment interest: Article 357, Article 468 of the Civil Code.
  • Personal income tax and registration fee: Article 14 of the PIT Law; Article 8 of consolidated text 33/VBHN-BTC.

See also: QS – quantities, payment, final settlement, case Q&A.

Results are for quick reference only, rounded to the nearest dong. The minimum advance payment, security and warranty rates are mandatory only for public investment, PPP or State-budget-funded projects, strictly within the scope of each article. PIT Law 109/2025/QH15 and Commercial Law 2005 are partially no longer in effect. Tax and fee calculation prices and exemptions must be checked against the specific case documents.
