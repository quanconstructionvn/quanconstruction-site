Turnkey

One partner, one journey, one complete home

You only need to work with a single company. Every stage has a person in charge and clear reporting.

1Listen & survey

We understand your family's needs, the land and the budget to choose the most suitable approach.

2Design

A living space that balances function, aesthetics and budget.

3Building permit

We prepare the dossier and handle permit procedures on your behalf, neatly and in line with regulations.

4Estimate & contract

A clear, transparent figure for every item, minimizing unexpected variations.

5Construction

Every layer of concrete and every steel joint is closely controlled for quality, safety and schedule.

6Finishing

The final touches, from aluminium-glass doors to fittings, are done with care.

7Acceptance & handover

We inspect every detail with the owner, settle accounts transparently and provide attentive warranty service.

8Move in

We hand over the keys and welcome you into your new home, and we stay with you after move-in day.
