Building Without or Contrary to a Permit: 2026 Fines in Vietnam

Quan Construction · 27/10/2026

From 26 August 2026, violations of construction order are sanctioned under Decree 339/2026/ND-CP, which replaces Decree 16/2022/ND-CP (Article 90). Violations that occurred and ended before this date remain subject to the old rules; violations that began earlier but are still ongoing are subject to the new decree (Article 89). This article summarizes what homeowners building private houses need to know.

1. How to read the fine levels correctly

Under Clause 4, Article 5 of Decree 339/2026/ND-CP, the fine levels stated in the decree apply to organizations; for the same act, an individual is fined half. The construction-order violations in Article 25 are not among the exceptions, so a homeowner who is an individual or household is subject to 1/2 of the figures in the article.

The limitation period for sanctioning in the construction sector is 2 years; for a private house, the time a violation ends is the date the house is put into use (Article 6).

2. How much is the fine for building without a permit?

Under Clause 7, Article 25, for constructing without a building permit where one is required:

  • Private houses: VND 60-80 million (organization level), i.e. VND 30-40 million for individuals.
  • Private houses in protection zones I and II of relics, world heritage areas or buffer zones: VND 80-100 million (individuals VND 40-50 million).

A house that falls within an exempt case is not fined for this violation. See also Which private houses are exempt from a building permit?

3. Building contrary to the permit

Also in Article 25, fines for private houses (outside relic and heritage areas) are as follows (organization / individual):

  • Contrary to a new-construction permit (Clause 6): VND 30-40 million / VND 15-20 million.
  • Contrary to a repair, renovation or relocation permit, or a fixed-term permit (Clause 4): VND 15-20 million / VND 7.5-10 million.
  • Reducing the construction area, height or number of floors compared with the permit in places where planning or the architectural regulation does not allow a reduction (Clause 3): VND 30-40 million / VND 15-20 million.

Important note in Clause 15, Article 25: if a change from the permit is not a case requiring a permit adjustment, it is not treated as building contrary to the permit. The changes that require applying for a permit adjustment (location, construction area, number of floors, main structure, exterior architecture in areas under architectural management, main functional divisions) are set out in Article 63 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP.

4. Other common violations by homeowners

  • Failing to cover the site and letting materials spill (Clause 1): VND 3-5 million (individuals VND 1.5-2.5 million).
  • Failing to display the permit publicly at the site (Clause 2): VND 5-10 million (individuals VND 2.5-5 million).
  • Unsafe construction causing settlement or cracks in adjoining buildings without harming health or life (Clause 5): VND 30-40 million (individuals VND 15-20 million).
  • Building contrary to approved planning (Clause 9), or extending or encroaching on others' space or public areas (Clause 10): VND 80-100 million (individuals VND 40-50 million), plus an order of demolition.
  • Continuing to build after a violation record has been made (Clause 11): VND 60-80 million; repeat violation (Clause 12): VND 80-100 million (individuals half). These two cases also incur revocation of the right to use the building permit and confiscation of the exhibits and means of the violation (Clause 13).

5. Stop-work order: 35 days to obtain a permit, otherwise demolition

Besides fines, a building without a permit or contrary to the permit that is under construction is handled under Article 85 of Decree 339/2026/ND-CP:

  1. If the house does not meet the conditions for a permit (or an adjusted permit): the violating part is ordered to be demolished.
  2. If it meets the conditions: construction is ordered to stop. Within 35 days from the date of the penalty decision, the homeowner must complete the application and be issued the permit (or adjusted permit); the application must include documents proving the fine has been paid.
  3. Once a permit is obtained: within a maximum of 5 days, the competent person inspects the current state. Construction may resume only when the current state conforms to the permit; any non-conforming part must be demolished by the owner within 15 days.
  4. After 35 days without presenting a permit: the owner receives a notice and must demolish the violating part within 15 days; failing that, enforcement will be applied.
  5. If construction continues while the permit application is pending: it is handled at the repeat-violation level under Clause 12, Article 25.

For a house already completed (the violation has ended), the remedial measure is demolition of the building or the violating part (Point d, Clause 14, Article 25). Parts built without a permit or contrary to the permit also cause difficulty when registering the house on the land use right certificate (sổ đỏ) - see As-built completion and registering the house on the land certificate.

6. How to avoid being fined

  • Check whether your house needs a permit and obtain the building permit before starting construction - see the procedure for a private house building permit.
  • Display the permit publicly at the site under Article 66 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP.
  • Build according to the permitted drawings; to change the number of floors, area, main structure, etc., apply for a permit adjustment before doing the work.
  • Cover the site and take measures to protect adjoining buildings; choose a capable contractor.
This article is only a summary for reference and does not replace the legal documents. Specific fines are decided by the competent person based on the case file; see the full text of Article 25 and Article 85 of Decree 339/2026/ND-CP.
