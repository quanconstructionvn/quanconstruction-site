Adjusting and Extending a Building Permit in Vietnam 2026

Quan Construction · 27/10/2026

You already have a building permit, but want to add a floor, change the structure, or have not been able to start construction. What should you do? From 1 July 2026, adjustment, extension and reissuance of building permits are handled under Article 63 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP. This article summarizes what homeowners need to know, with links to the original text.

1. The start deadline stated in the permit

Under Clause 10, Article 49 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP, a building permit states a start deadline of no more than 12 months from the date the permit is issued or extended. So if you cannot start after receiving the permit (for lack of funds, while choosing a contractor, etc.), keep track of this date so you can apply for an extension in time.

2. When must you apply to adjust the building permit?

Under Clause 1, Article 63, during construction, if a design adjustment changes any of the following, the investor must apply to adjust the permit:

  • A change to the exterior architectural form, for works in areas with architectural management requirements;
  • A change to the building's location, construction area, number of floors or main structural solution;
  • An internal design adjustment that changes the division of the main functional uses inside the building.

If a design adjustment does not change the items above, the permit does not need to be adjusted.

3. Application for permit adjustment

Under Clause 2, Article 63, the application includes:

  1. An application to adjust the building permit, Form No. 02 in Appendix II of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP;
  2. The issued building permit with the drawing set issued with it;
  3. The adjusted construction design drawings;
  4. The appraisal result report and the approval document for the adjusted construction design under Decree 217/2026/ND-CP, including content on structural safety, fire prevention and fighting, and environmental protection;
  5. Lawful land documents under Article 55, if the adjustment changes the land use area or land use function.

The actual contents of the application depend on each building; homeowners should ask the One-Stop Department of the commune or ward People's Committee beforehand to prepare correctly and avoid repeated supplementation.

4. Permit extension: up to 2 times, 12 months each

Under Clause 3, Article 63, if by the time the permit's start validity expires the building has not yet started, the investor must apply for an extension. Each permit may be extended at most 2 times, 12 months each time.

The extension application is simple and consists of:

  • An application for extension of the building permit, Form No. 02 in Appendix II;
  • The original or a certified copy of the building permit, or the issued electronic permit.

The adjustment or extension is recorded directly on the original issued permit or issued as a supplementary appendix attached to the permit. If the permit is torn, damaged or lost, apply for reissuance under Article 64.

5. Where to apply and how long it takes

Under Clause 4, Article 53, the authority that issued the permit is the one that adjusts, extends and reissues the permits it issued; for private houses of households and individuals this is usually the commune-level People's Committee. Under Article 54, applications are submitted online through the National Public Service Portal (in person or via public postal service only when an incident makes online submission impossible). Time limits from receipt of a complete and valid application:

  • Adjustment of a private house permit: 7 working days;
  • Extension or reissuance of a permit: 5 working days;
  • If the application is incomplete, the licensing authority notifies once; the homeowner supplements within 2 working days (adjustment) or 1 working day (extension, reissuance).

For the first-time permit application, see Procedure for a private house building permit 2026.

6. What if you build differently from the permit without adjusting it?

Under Clause 6, Article 25 of Decree 339/2026/ND-CP, constructing a private house contrary to the content of a new-construction permit incurs a fine of VND 30,000,000 to 40,000,000 (VND 50,000,000 to 70,000,000 in relic and heritage protection areas under Point b). However, under Clause 15 of the same Article, if the construction differs from the permit but is not a case requiring permit adjustment, it is not treated as a violation and is not sanctioned.

In addition, under Article 65, a permit may be revoked if the investor fails to remedy construction contrary to the permit within the period required by the competent authority.

This article is only a summary for reference and does not replace the legal documents. Before changing the design, consult the design firm and the One-Stop Department of the commune or ward People's Committee where the building is located.
