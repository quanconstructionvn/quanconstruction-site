Letter from the Director

Professional, dedicated, accompanying every project

Dear Authorities, Investors and Partners,

Quan Construction Investment and Development Company Limited operates professionally in consulting, design and construction of civil and industrial buildings. With engineers, architects and technical staff of high expertise and practical experience, we always provide integrated, efficient construction solutions that comply with current standards and legislation.

We place particular importance on strict compliance with construction standards, national technical regulations, construction investment rules, occupational safety and environmental protection. We look forward to the opportunity to work with you on construction projects and to create sustainable, modern buildings that serve the community and society.
