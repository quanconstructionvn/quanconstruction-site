Fire Safety Design Appraisal 2026: Which Works Need It in Vietnam

Quan Construction · 10/11/2026

Before new construction, renovation or change of use, the investor needs to know whether the building requires fire prevention and fighting (fire safety) design appraisal. The basis is Article 17 of the 2024 Fire Law, Decree 105/2025/ND-CP and the new Appendix III issued with Decree 347/2026/ND-CP (effective from 15 September 2026).

1. What is fire safety design appraisal and why does it matter?

The design of a building must include fire safety solutions: fire separation distances; roads and parking areas for fire engines; escape; fire resistance rating and fire spread prevention; smoke control; electricity for fire safety; and fire safety equipment and systems (Clause 1, Article 16).

For a building subject to appraisal, the investor or owner may carry out construction or renovation only after obtaining the fire safety design appraisal document from the specialized management authority (Clause 3, Article 17).

2. Which works must be appraised? (new Appendix III)

From 15 September 2026, Appendix III of Decree 105/2025/ND-CP is replaced by the Appendix III issued with Decree 347/2026/ND-CP (Clause 1, Article 31). Some common groups (see the full text of Appendix III at the end of the document):

  • Housing: apartment buildings and collective housing of 7 floors or more or with a total floor area of 3,000 m² or more.
  • Hotels, guesthouses, accommodation facilities; offices and administrative buildings: 7 floors or more or a total floor area of 3,000 m² or more.
  • Karaoke, nightclubs, recreation and entertainment works: 4 floors or more or a total floor area of 1,000 m² or more.
  • Commercial: shopping centers, supermarkets, markets and shops trading flammable goods of 2,000 m² floor area or more; restaurants and eateries of 3,000 m² floor area or more.
  • Education and healthcare: nurseries and kindergartens with 150 children or more or 2,000 m² floor area or more; clinics, elderly care facilities, etc. of 5 floors or more or 2,000 m² floor area or more.
  • Petrol stations, internal fuel supply stations with 1 or more pumps; enclosed car and motorbike parking or showroom buildings of 2,000 m² floor area or more.

Total floor area is determined by the largest building of the project or work (note to Appendix III).

3. Does a private house need fire safety design appraisal?

The new Appendix III does not list private houses, and its mixed-use works item states explicitly excluding private houses combined with production or business. So family homes, including homes used for both living and trading, are not on the list requiring appraisal.

The house must still meet fire safety conditions - see Fire safety requirements for homes 2026 and Home-based businesses: fire safety conditions. If building a guesthouse, karaoke venue, nursery, etc., compare against the thresholds in section 2.

4. Who appraises?

  • The specialized construction authority: appraises the separation distances, fire-fighting traffic, escape, fire resistance rating and smoke control parts when appraising the Feasibility Study Report (Clause 2, Article 93 of the Construction Law 2025 amending Point b, Clause 1, Article 17 of the Fire Law; Article 12 of Decree 347/2026/ND-CP).
  • The investor or owner: self-appraises the above parts if they are not within the authority of the specialized construction authority, and prepares the result in Form PC13 (Clause 1, Article 8 of Decree 105/2025/ND-CP).
  • The Police authority: appraises the electricity for fire safety and fire safety equipment and systems such as alarms, fire fighting and emergency lighting (Clause 2, Article 9). Most works are appraised by the provincial Police Department of Fire Prevention, Fighting, Rescue (Article 9 of Circular 36/2025/TT-BCA).

5. Documents, time limit and fee at the Police authority

The application consists of (Point a, Clause 4, Article 9, as amended by Article 14 of Decree 347/2026/ND-CP):

  1. A written request for fire safety design appraisal in Form PC11 (new form issued with Decree 347/2026/ND-CP);
  2. Lawful land documents or a document on the investment policy or construction of the work;
  3. Technical design or construction drawing documents showing the electrical system for fire safety and the fire safety equipment and systems.
  • The application is certified by the investor and the design consultant; 1 set is submitted in person, by post or online (Clauses 4 and 5, Article 9).
  • Time limit: no more than 6 working days from receipt of a complete and valid application; for nationally important and Group A projects, no more than 10 days (Clause 6, Article 9).
  • Fee: total investment (excluding compensation, support, resettlement and land use costs) multiplied by the fee rate; minimum VND 500,000, maximum VND 150,000,000 per project (Article 4 of Circular 70/2025/TT-BTC).

6. Renovation or change of use: when is re-appraisal needed?

A work subject to appraisal whose design is adjusted, or whose use is changed or that is renovated in a way that changes fire safety conditions, must be re-appraised (Clause 2, Article 17). For example:

  • Adding floors; lowering the fire resistance rating; reducing the number of escape routes or changing the type or location of escape staircases; changing the main functional divisions (Clause 4, Article 6).
  • Changing the principle of the fire alarm or fire fighting system or the parameters of fire pumps; replacing or adding a fire alarm or fire fighting system (Clause 3, Article 9).
This article is only a summary for reference and does not replace the legal documents. The 2024 Fire Law has been amended (Law 118/2025/QH15, Construction Law 2025); Decree 105/2025/ND-CP has been amended by Decree 347/2026/ND-CP. Applications submitted before 15 September 2026 are handled under the rules in force at the time of receipt (Article 40 of Decree 347/2026/ND-CP). Ask the provincial Police Department of Fire Prevention, Fighting, Rescue before designing.
