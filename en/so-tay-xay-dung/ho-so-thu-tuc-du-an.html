This page gathers the main legal procedures for a construction project or works under the Construction Law 2025 and the 2026 decrees. For the overall sequence, see the Project sequence handbook.

2. File for appraisal of the Feasibility Study Report

Under Article 35 of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP, a file is valid when it has the required components in the proper format, is mainly in Vietnamese, and has been checked and confirmed by the project preparation agency; online submission must be digitally signed. Main components:

  • Submission for appraisal under Form No. 01 of Appendix I of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP.
  • Document on investment policy or document approving project information (or equivalent legal papers for transitional projects).
  • Document approving the architectural plan through a design competition (if required).
  • The Feasibility Study Report file, survey file, basic design/FEED and other legal documents under Clause 2, Article 35.

Matters appraised by the specialized agency: conformity with planning, infrastructure connectivity, compliance with construction regulations and safety, fire prevention and fighting solutions, and compliance with cost management regulations for public investment and PPP projects (Clause 4, Article 27 of the Construction Law 2025).

3. Design verification and appraisal

  • Mandatory verification for works that significantly affect safety or community interests, or are large-scale and technically complex, and works subject to fire prevention and fighting design appraisal (Clause 5, Article 26 of the Construction Law; Clause 3, Article 29).
  • Investor appraisal of the detailed design step immediately before selecting the construction / EPC, EC, EP contractor; for the other steps the investor decides on the level of control (Clause 1, Art. 41, Decree 217).
  • Approval by decision under Form No. 13, with the approval stamp under Form No. 14 of Appendix I placed on the explanatory report and drawings; may be delegated to the PMU unless a project management consultant is hired (Art. 42, Decree 217).
  • The construction cost estimate is appraised together with the design; after approval the investor updates it in the database (Article 16, Article 17, Decree 206).

4. Building permits

Three types of permit: new construction; repair, renovation and relocation; and temporary (Clause 1, Art. 43, Construction Law). Many works are exempt from permits, notably works of projects whose Feasibility Study Report has been appraised by the specialized construction agency and approved (Point e, Clause 2, Article 43).

Permit conditions: conformity with the land use purpose; conformity with planning, urban design or architectural management regulations (except temporary permits); a design that ensures safety, environment, fire prevention and fighting and infrastructure connection (Article 44). The file for non-linear / linear works consists of (Clause 1, Art. 57, Decree 217):

  1. Application under Form No. 01 of Appendix II of Decree 217/2026/ND-CP.
  2. Lawful land papers under Article 55, or a document approving the location and route plan, or a land recovery decision.
  3. Project approval decision; verification report (if any); results of fire prevention and fighting and environmental procedures; opinion of the provincial-level cultural agency (if required).
  4. Set of drawings: general layout, positioning, main plans – elevations – sections (or route diagram, plan view, longitudinal/cross sections), foundation plan and section, main structural solutions, infrastructure connections.

Files are submitted online on the National Public Service Portal; drawings carry the digital signature of the designing organization or individual; land papers already in the national database need not be submitted (Article 56). If the file is incomplete, the permit-issuing agency notifies once; the investor supplements within 02 working days (Clause 2, Article 54). For individual houses, see the article procedure for an individual house permit.

5. Commencement and management during construction

  • Send the commencement notice in the Appendix V form of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP, directly, by post or online (Clause 2, Article 12).
  • Publicly display the building permit at the site (Art. 66, Decree 217); meet site requirements (Art. 49, Construction Law; Art. 13, Decree 207).
  • If a design change alters the permit content, adjust the permit (Art. 63, Decree 217).

6. Fire prevention and fighting: appraisal and acceptance

For projects subject to fire prevention and fighting (FPF) design appraisal: distances, fire-fighting access, evacuation, fire resistance rating, fire compartmentation and smoke control are appraised by the specialized construction agency together with the Feasibility Study Report (Clause 2, Article 93 of the Construction Law 2025, amending Article 17 of the Fire Prevention Law); FPF systems and equipment are appraised by the Police authority at the technical design / construction drawing step (Article 9 of Decree 105/2025).

FPF acceptance: the investor organizes acceptance of each part, item, system and the whole; concealed parts must be accepted before being covered (Article 18 of the Fire Prevention Law). From 15 September 2026, Decree 347/2026/ND-CP repeals Article 10 of Decree 105/2025/ND-CP (the procedure for the Police authority to inspect FPF acceptance work) and provides that the investor organizes acceptance, is responsible for the results and declares on the FPF Database before putting the works into operation (Art. 16, Decree 347).

7. Acceptance, inspection of acceptance work, putting into use

  • The investor organizes completion acceptance when: construction is complete per design; work-item and stage acceptance is complete; test, inspection and trial-run results are satisfactory; and fire prevention and environmental requirements are met (Clause 1, Art. 24, Decree 207).
  • Works subject to inspection (nationally important, large-scale or technically complex projects per Appendix IX; works that significantly affect safety or community interests) must have their acceptance work inspected by the competent authority (Article 25; authority: Article 26).
  • Inspection during construction is limited to 03 times (special grade, Grade I works) or 02 times (others); on completion the investor sends a completion report under Appendix VI (Article 27).
  • Works may be put into operation only after acceptance; works subject to inspection must have a document approving the acceptance results; an environmental permit/registration is required if applicable (Art. 29, Decree 207).

8. Completion files and record keeping

The investor prepares the works completion file under Appendix VII of Decree 207/2026/ND-CP and is responsible for its accuracy; contractors are responsible for the parts of the file they prepare (Article 28). Note:

  • Prepared in Vietnamese; where there is a foreign element, English or another language is also agreed.
  • Prepared as one file for the whole project if put into use at the same time, or separately for each work if at different times.
  • Keep originals and electronic copies; where there is no original, use the master copy or a lawful copy.
  • Before handover, the construction contractor delivers as-built drawings, operation procedures, maintenance procedures, and the list of equipment and spare parts (Clause 3, Art. 58, Construction Law).
  • Files serving management and use are kept by the managing and operating owner for at least the service life of the works (Clause 2, Article 42).

For the detailed list of quality files and forms, see Quality and acceptance files and Forms.

This is a summary for reference and does not replace legal documents or the guidance of the handling agency. The full text of the forms in Appendices I, II, V, VI and VII of Decrees 207/2026 and 217/2026 is not yet in the library. The Urban and Rural Planning Law, the Fire Prevention Law and Decree 105/2025/ND-CP are partly no longer in force; the amending document, Law 118/2025/QH15 on fire prevention, is not yet in the library.
